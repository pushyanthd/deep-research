# Samsung S90D 65-inch US buyer brief for mixed movies and PS5 gaming

## Executive summary

Verdict: the 65-inch Samsung S90D is a strong fit for mixed movies and PS5 gaming in moderate ambient light if you want OLED contrast, rich color, and fast gaming more than format completeness. CNET and WIRED describe excellent blacks/contrast, vivid yet realistic color, effective reflection handling, and responsive HDMI 2.1 gaming, while Choose.tv reports a QD-OLED 65-inch unit. [S1] [S2] [S3]

Inference: the main trade-off is that the S90D gives up Dolby Vision and DTS support, and bright-room black depth can fall behind darker-room viewing even when reflections are controlled. Because the S90D family varies by size and region, the 65-inch US result should not be generalized to every S90D size. [S1] [S2] [S3]

## Methodology

Reviewed only the approved claims and excerpts, prioritized independent measurements and official feature specs where available, and omitted unsupported pricing claims.

## Buyer verdict for mixed movies and PS5 gaming

Inference: Buy the 65-inch S90D if your priority is a movie-first OLED that still feels quick and capable for PS5 play. CNET found the 65-inch model looked excellent in dark-room movie and game use, while WIRED described it as bright for an OLED with perfect blacks, excellent shadow detail, and an effective antireflective screen. [S1] [S2]

Inference: Skip it as a default if Dolby Vision, DTS, or Chromecast are must-haves, or if you care more about preserving the deepest black levels in brighter rooms than about Samsung’s OLED color and gaming advantages. The trade-off is better glare/reflection control than some glossy OLED rivals, but some contrast loss in more ambient light. [S1] [S2]

## Picture quality and gaming performance

Independent reviews converge on strong image quality: deep blacks, strong contrast, vivid color, excellent shadow detail, and very good off-axis viewing. CNET said the dark-room differences versus the LG C4 were subtle, while WIRED said the picture is nearly unbeatable for the money and especially engaging in HDR. [S1] [S2]

On gaming, Samsung’s published specs list four HDMI 2.1 ports with ALLM and VRR, up to 144 Hz for supported PC games, and 120 Hz with PS5/Xbox Series S|X; CNET also reported low Game Mode lag and easy Game Hub switching into Game Mode. [S1] [S4]

The main downside in mixed use is room brightness: CNET says the S90D can lose some contrast in a bright room, and WIRED notes that while reflections are handled well, the TV is not the brightest or most contrast-preserving OLED in Samsung’s lineup. [S1] [S2]

## Panel variant implications for the US 65-inch model

The 65-inch US test unit is the panel variant to trust here: CNET says the 65-inch uses QD-OLED, while the 42-, 48-, and 83-inch S90D sizes use WOLED instead, so those sizes should not inherit the 65-inch results. [S1]

SamMobile adds that S90D panel type can vary by region and size, so a buyer should verify the exact SKU rather than assuming every S90D is the same. Inference: for this report, the 65-inch verdict is specific to the cited US model and should not be generalized beyond it. [S5] [S1]

## Value, launch pricing, verified current offers, and key drawbacks

No verified US launch price or live offer is available in the approved materials, so price-based value ranking is withheld. [S1] [S2]

The key drawbacks are format omissions and room-dependent contrast: CNET notes no Dolby Vision, WIRED also flags no DTS and no Chromecast, and CNET/Choose.tv say the set can lose some black-depth punch in brighter rooms even though reflections are handled well. [S1] [S2] [S3]

## Conclusion

Verdict: the 65-inch S90D is a buy for a US viewer who wants a bright-enough OLED for mixed movies and PS5 gaming in moderate ambient light; the evidence supports excellent blacks, vivid color, low-lag HDMI 2.1 gaming, and decent reflection handling. [S1] [S2] [S3]

Inference: pass only if Dolby Vision, DTS, or Chromecast are deal-breakers, or if you need the very best bright-room black depth; verify the exact 65-inch panel/SKU before purchase because S90D variants differ by size and region. [S1] [S2] [S5]
